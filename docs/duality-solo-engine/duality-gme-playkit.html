The Play Loop

  1. Create your character with the Daggerheart rules.
  2. Swear an Iron Vow on an inciting incident.
  3. Begin a Session each time you sit down.
  4. Envision, then make moves. Most are an action roll with the trait that fits.
  5. Read the outcome: strong hit, weak hit or miss. On a miss, Pay the Price.
  6. Take or suffer Momentum. Mark progress on your tracks; roll progress to finish them.
  7. End a Session when you stop.

Critical successStrong hit + critical benefits
Success with HopeStrong hit
Success with FearWeak hit: at a cost

Hope and Fear are gained as usual.

Momentum

−6 to +10, starts at +2. Take +N up to your maximum; suffer −N down to −6. At −6, more loss is Face a Setback.

Burn it after an action roll when it is higher than your Fear die: ignore the Fear die and improve the result one step (miss to weak, weak to strong); no Fear for the world, +1 Hope for you. Then reset: +2, +1 with one debility, 0 with more. Not on a progress roll.

Negative Momentum that matches your Hope die cancels it: take its number off the total.

Initiative (fights): a strong hit takes or keeps it; a weak hit or a miss gives it to your foe.

Ranks & Progress

Each track has 10 boxes of 4 ticks. The rank sets what one mark progress fills and the Difficulty of rolls against it. A bond is always 1 tick.

Progress roll: roll two d10 against your progress score (full boxes). Beat both: strong hit. Beat one: weak hit. Neither: miss. Ties go to the dice. Matching dice: a twist. No Hope or Fear.

Set the Difficulty

Use a Difficulty the rules, the adversary or the rank already give. Otherwise start at 13 and add each obstacle already true in the scene:

Say it before you roll; don't change it after.

Ask the Oracle

Pick a likelihood, roll the Duality Dice. Yes on the total shown. Doubles: extreme answer or twist. Open questions: roll Meaning (page 6).

Pay the Price

Take the obvious cost, or roll. Severe: roll twice, both happen.

Begin a Session

  1. Review your flags.
  2. Recap where you left off.
  3. Set the scene and your intent.
  4. Optional: roll a Vignette elsewhere, then take +1 momentum.

Fate Moves

Foes

A foe's rank sets the harm it deals you and the progress each harm you inflict marks. A Daggerheart adversary's rank is tier − 1 + role bonus.

A small pack (3 to 5 Minor or Serious foes) is one rank higher; a large pack (6 to 10) two. Your harm: 1 + half Proficiency (up), or 1 unarmed.

Stats and Traits

A +1 in Ironsworn is +2 here.

Debilities

Each one lowers your maximum Momentum and your reset by 1.

Supply and Levels

Supply is 0 to 5. At 0, mark Unprepared. You level up at a milestone (a Grave or greater vow, a story arc): Advance. Conditions are on the character sheet. Health and spirit are your Hit Points and Stress.

Meaning

Hope die: row, Fear die: column (odd A, even B). Action + Subject for events, Descriptor + Subject for things.
