The Play Loop

  1. Create your character with the Daggerheart rules.
  2. Take an Oath on an inciting incident.
  3. Begin a Session each time you sit down.
  4. Envision, then make moves. Most are an action roll with the trait that fits.
  5. Read the outcome: strong hit, weak hit or miss. On a miss, Pay the Price.
  6. Mark progress on your tracks; roll progress to finish them.
  7. End a Session when you stop.

Action Roll Outcomes

Daggerheart resultMove outcome
Critical successStrong hit + critical benefits
Success with HopeStrong hit
Success with FearWeak hit: at a cost
Failure with HopeMiss: Pay the Price
Failure with FearMiss; GM may spend 1 Fear: severe

Hope and Fear are gained as usual.

Ranks & Progress

Each track has 10 boxes of 4 ticks. The rank sets what one mark progress fills and the Difficulty of rolls against it.

Progress roll: roll two d10 against your progress score (full boxes). Beat both: strong hit. Beat one: weak hit. Neither: miss. Ties go to the dice. Matching dice: a twist. No Hope or Fear.

Set the Difficulty

Use a Difficulty the rules, the adversary or the rank already give. Otherwise start at 13 and add each obstacle already true in the scene:

ObstaclesDifficulty
None (simple: 10)13
One15
Two18
Resistance + Demand + Conditions20

Say it before you roll; don't change it after.

Ask the Oracle

Pick a likelihood, roll the Duality Dice. Yes on the total shown. Doubles: extreme answer or twist. Open questions: roll Meaning (page 3).

Pay the Price

Take the obvious cost, or roll. Severe: roll twice, both happen.

Begin a Session

  1. Review your flags.
  2. Recap where you left off.
  3. Set the scene and your intent.
  4. Optional: roll a Vignette elsewhere, then gain a Hope.

Connection Moves

Scene Challenge

Name the objective: a grave track and a tension clock of 4 (disadvantage), 6 (even) or 8 (advantage) segments.

Combat Moves

GM turn: pick the adversary that fits, make a Fear Check. Fail: it acts free, roll Tactics. Pass: roll Fear Spend, pay it. Chain until a check fails.

Fate Moves

Meaning

Hope die: row, Fear die: column (odd A, even B). Action + Subject for events, Descriptor + Subject for things.
