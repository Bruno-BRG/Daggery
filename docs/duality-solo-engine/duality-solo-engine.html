Duality Solo Engine

A d12 game master emulator for solo & GM-less Daggerheart. The Fear pool plays the GM. Version 2.0.

How to Play

  1. Create your character with the Daggerheart rules.
  2. Roll a starting Plot Hook and a Random Event, then Set the Scene.
  3. Ask the Oracle whenever you would ask a GM. Interpret answers in context.
  4. Play the game to overcome the scene: roll action rolls as normal, and roll an Encounter when trouble turns to a fight.
  5. Use GM Moves to move the action when you roll with Fear, fail, or stall.
  6. Set the Scene for the next thing your character wants to do.

Take the first interpretation that makes sense. If nothing fits, the obvious thing happens. Keep Threads (open goals) and NPCs on lists of 12 or fewer, so a d12 can pick from them.

Fear & the Fear Check

Track Fear as the rules say: it grows when you roll with Fear and when you rest, to a maximum of 12. The engine never invents Fear; it decides when to spend what you generate.

Fear Check: roll a d12. Equal to or under current Fear: it passes, the GM spends Fear and acts. Otherwise it fails. At 0 Fear it always fails.

1Plot Hook

Why are you here? Roll once per column. Keep the Twist hidden until a Random Event or Hard Move reveals it.

2Random Event

Roll the Event Focus, then Action + Subject. Also on Oracle doubles and Interrupted scenes. Focus 4–6: roll your NPC list; 7–9: your Thread list.

Rumors & Leads

3Set the Scene

Describe where your character is and what they are trying to accomplish. Roll a Scene Complication. Then make a Fear Check:

  • Fails: the scene plays with its complication.
  • Passes, odd roll: spend 1 Fear. The scene is Altered: also roll Altered Scene.
  • Passes, even roll: spend 1 Fear. The scene is Interrupted: a Random Event replaces it.

4The Oracle (Yes / No)

Ask a yes/no question, pick a likelihood, roll your Hope and Fear dice and add them. Oracle rolls never generate Hope or Fear.

Twist: dice 4 or more apart twist the answer. Hope higher: your way (Yes, and… / No, but…). Fear higher: against you (Yes, but… / No, and…). Doubles: answer, then a Random Event.

Oracle (How Much? How Well?)

5GM Moves

  • Success with Fear or failure with Hope: Soft Move.
  • Failure with Fear: Fear Check. Pass: spend 1 Fear, Hard Move. Fail: Soft Move.
  • Story stalls or a golden opportunity: Fear Check. Pass: spend 1 Fear, Hard Move. Fail: nothing.

Not every failure needs a move: use one when failure has consequences or the action needs to pick up.

6Next Scene

When the scene is resolved, update your lists and Set the Scene for what your character does next. Rest and downtime add Fear as the rules say.

Meaning (Open Questions)

For what a yes/no cannot answer. Roll both Duality Dice per word: the Hope die picks the row, the Fear die picks the column (odd = A, even = B). Action + Subject for events, Descriptor + Subject for things and places.

Generic Generator

For anything else (a room, a relic, a rumor, a town): Descriptor + Subject for what it is, Action for what it does, How Much for its size or strength.

Countdowns

Give every looming threat a countdown. Soft and Hard Moves tick it; at zero it happens, with no Fear needed.

Encounters

When trouble turns to a fight, roll Creature and Situation, then the Adversary Type: the Daggerheart role it plays. Pick a stat block of that type and your tier (reskin one to fit the creature) and build the fight with Battle Points. Ask the Oracle whether they notice you first.

Adversaries in Combat

When the spotlight passes to the GM, pick the adversary that makes the most sense (or that has acted least) and make a Fear Check.

  • Fails: it acts for free. Roll Tactics.
  • Passes: roll Fear Spend, pay the cost, resolve it.
  • Chain: after each GM action, another Fear Check. Pass: spend 1 Fear and spotlight another adversary (Tactics). The GM turn ends on the first failed check.

A Fear feature costs what its stat block says; the other results cost 1 Fear. If the engine cannot pay, it rolls Tactics instead.

Names

For a new NPC: the Hope die picks the given name, the Fear die the column (odd A, even B). Add a Family name, an Epithet, or both.

Hazards & Traps

Make the reaction roll named. Difficulty and damage by tier.

Loot & Treasure

After a fight or in a vault, roll Loot and a Detail that makes it a story.

NPC Generator

Roll once per column. Choose ancestry and community to fit the place.

Attitude is the first reaction. On their own, NPCs follow their Motive; when unsure, ask the Oracle. Topic is what they bring up, ask about, or let slip.

Rest & Camp

Site Crawler

For dungeons, ruins, and lairs. For each new area roll Area, Exits, and Contents. The first time you roll Area 12, you have found the heart of the site.

Tips

  • Ask mostly yes/no questions.
  • Give every result meaning; reject the nonsensical.
  • Use GM Moves to drive the action.
  • Write what you need where you like: the tables are the engine.

Settlements

Name it with one First and one Second part (Ash + ford: Ashford), then roll Size, Known for, and its Trouble: a ready hook.

Journeys

For each leg of travel make a Fear Check. Pass: spend 1 Fear and roll Danger. Fail: roll Discovery.

Wilds

For each new region or day of travel: the land, the weather, and a landmark to steer by.

Factions

Who they are, what they want, and how they get it. Add the ones that matter to your NPCs or Threads.
