Duality Solo Engine

A d12 game master emulator for solo & GM-less Daggerheart. The Fear pool plays the GM.

How to Play

  1. Frame the scene you expect, then test it (Scene Setup).
  2. Play your character with the normal rules. Whenever you would ask a GM something, ask the Oracle.
  3. When you fail or roll with Fear, the engine makes a GM Move.
  4. When the answer needs inspiration, roll on the Meaning tables and interpret the words in context.
  5. End the scene: update your lists of Threads (open goals) and NPCs. Keep each list to 12 or fewer so a d12 can pick from it.

Always take the first interpretation that makes sense. If nothing fits, the obvious thing happens.

Fear & the Fear Check

Track Fear exactly as the rules say: it grows when you roll with Fear and when you rest, to a maximum of 12. The engine never invents Fear. It only decides when to spend what you generate.

Fear Check: roll a d12. If it is equal to or under current Fear, the check passes: the GM spends Fear and acts. Otherwise it fails and the world holds its breath. At 0 Fear it always fails.

The Oracle (Yes / No)

Ask a yes/no question, pick a likelihood, roll your Hope and Fear dice and add them. Oracle rolls never generate Hope or Fear.

Twist: if the dice are 4 or more apart, add a twist. Hope higher: it bends your way (Yes, and… / No, but…). Fear higher: it bends against you (Yes, but… / No, and…).
Doubles: answer as normal, then a Random Event occurs.

How Much? How Well?

Scene Setup

Describe the scene you expect, then make a Fear Check.

  • Fails: the scene starts as expected.
  • Passes on an odd roll: spend 1 Fear. The scene is Altered: roll below.
  • Passes on an even roll: spend 1 Fear. The scene is Interrupted: a Random Event replaces it.

GM Moves

  • Success with Fear or failure with Hope: Soft Move.
  • Failure with Fear: Fear Check. Pass: spend 1 Fear, Hard Move. Fail: Soft Move.
  • Story stalls or you hand the world a golden opportunity: Fear Check. Pass: spend 1 Fear, Hard Move.

Random Event

Triggered by Oracle doubles or an Interrupted scene. Roll the Event Focus, then Action + Subject for what happens. For a Thread or NPC, roll a d12 on your list (reroll blanks) or choose.

Meaning Tables

Roll both Duality Dice. The Hope die picks the row; the Fear die picks the column (odd = A, even = B). Combine Action + Subject for events, Descriptor + Subject for things and places.

Adversaries in Combat

When the spotlight passes to the GM, pick the adversary that makes the most sense (or the one that has acted least) and make a Fear Check.

  • Fails: it acts for free. Roll Tactics.
  • Passes: roll Fear Spend, pay the cost, resolve it.
  • Chain: after each GM action, make another Fear Check. On a pass, spend 1 Fear and spotlight another adversary (Tactics). The GM turn ends on the first failed check.

Build encounters with Battle Points as normal. If the engine cannot afford a cost, it rolls Tactics instead.

Generic Generator

For anything else (a room, a relic, a rumor, a town): roll Descriptor + Subject for what it is, Action for what it does, and How Much for its size or strength.

Countdowns

Give every looming threat a countdown die. Soft and Hard Moves tick it; at zero it happens, with no Fear needed. A d12 on your Thread list picks which countdown ticks when it is unclear.

NPC Generator

Roll once per column. Choose ancestry and community to fit the place.

Attitude is the first reaction. An NPC acting on their own follows their Motive; when unsure, ask the Oracle. Topic is what they bring up, ask about, or let slip.

Plot Hook Generator

Roll once per column. Keep the Twist hidden: reveal it when a Random Event or Hard Move gives it an opening.

Site Crawler

For dungeons, ruins, and lairs. For each new area roll Area, Exits, and Contents. The first time you roll Area 12, you have found the heart of the site.

Journeys

For each leg of travel make a Fear Check. Pass: spend 1 Fear and roll Danger. Fail: roll Discovery.
